# DARE TO FLY – MOXON ANTENNA PROJECT FOR 10 M

Guide to building an HF antenna for **10 meters** (also resonant on **15 and 20 m**), inspired by the design of radio amateur **Toivo, W8TJM**.  
[Video reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg-1zO1MJS8&t=95s)

## Introduction
The **Moxon antenna** is well known in the amateur radio world for its simplicity, efficiency, and excellent performance in limited spaces. Its rectangular design, with ends folded inward, provides a defined directional pattern and a remarkable front‑to‑back ratio, all with accessible and easy‑to‑handle materials.

Radio amateur **Toivo, W8TJM** popularized practical and economical variants of the Moxon, showing that with creativity and precision it is possible to achieve a high‑quality antenna without complex structures. This guide takes his contributions as a reference and adapts them to a homemade approach, aimed at those who enjoy experimenting and building with their own hands.

The goal is that, by the end, you will have an antenna ready to operate on the **28 MHz band**, optimized for DX and local contacts, combining portability with performance.

From my own experience, I was able to multiply the range I had on 40 meters by **4 to 6 times** compared to a modern directional antenna. Highly recommended for both performance and cost.

---

## 🛠️ Required Materials
- **PL‑259 connectors**  
- **4 mm copper wire** for the radiating elements and the reflector (using different colors, e.g., red and blue, is recommended)  
- **Four fishing rods** of 3 m for the arms  
- A **wooden boom**  
- **10 cm spacers** to join the radiators and reflector  
- Clamps and plastic ties  
- Plywood pieces painted for waterproofing and protection against humidity and weather
